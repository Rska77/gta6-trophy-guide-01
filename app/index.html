Before you start

  • Keep manual saves. Story choices and a handful of timed encounters are permanent; a save beside them costs nothing.
  • Do the finale last. Some contacts and side moments close after the endgame. Clean up first, then finish the story.
  • Let counters settle. Trophies can lag a few seconds behind a mission banner. Check the stats page before replaying anything.
  • Overlap your grinds. WAiNK check-ins, vehicle discovery and area exploration all happen on the same drive.
